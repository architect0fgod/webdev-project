Team Name: DocktypeHtml

Pippo: Student Focus, Schedule & Diary Hub is a responsive, browser-based productivity companion built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript to combat study burnout and context-switching fatigue among college students. Currently, students rely on fragmented workflows—juggling separate browser tabs or apps for focus timers (such as Pomofocus or Forest), deadline tracking (Google Calendar), and personal reflection—which disrupts concentration and requires constant internet connectivity or account sign-ups. Pippo solves this problem by unifying a customizable Pomodoro study timer, built-in offline royalty-free lo-fi ambient audio, an interactive date-based note-taking calendar, and a mood-tracking daily diary inside a single distraction-free interface. Unlike existing tools that either focus solely on countdown timers without academic note-taking or gate features behind subscriptions and cloud logins, Pippo runs 100% offline in the browser using localStorage
